## Case Study on BFE DCO - Incrementality & A/B Performance Evaluation
- date: 2025
- tools: 
  - SQL
  - Tableau
  - Excel
  - A/B Testing
- description: Led an independent data-driven evaluation of Dynamic Creative Optimization (DCO) versus static creative metrics across multi-million impression programmatic datasets. Discovered that a reported 80% uplift was phase-bound rather than incremental against control variables, resulting in major strategic adjustments.
- results:
  - Prevented substantial ad-tech creative budget misallocation by identifying zero statistically meaningful performance variance (~0.5% CPBFE variance).
  - Delivered actionable deployment frameworks regarding audience segmentation and creative scaling rules to executive stakeholders.
